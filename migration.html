The Annual Cycle

Nature follows rainfall, not a timetable.

The migration is a living cycle rather than a single event. Our Kenya-based team uses current rainfall, grass conditions and ranger reports to place guests in the strongest wildlife areas for their dates.

Chronological Movement

Following the Ancient Rhythm

January – March

Calving on the Southern Plains

Hundreds of thousands of calves arrive across the nutrient-rich Serengeti and Ngorongoro plains. New life draws cheetah, lion and hyena, creating exceptional predator encounters and dramatic photography.

  • Best for newborn wildlife and predators
  • Green landscapes and softer visitor numbers
  • Combine with Ngorongoro Crater
Request This Window
April – June

The Northbound Journey

As the long rains refresh the central and western Serengeti, the herds form extraordinary columns and begin moving toward the Grumeti River. This is a beautifully atmospheric, quieter time to travel.

  • Long columns across sweeping plains
  • Excellent value at premium camps
  • Dramatic skies and lush scenery
Plan a Green-Season Safari
July – October

The Mara River Crossings

The herds gather along the Mara River and cross unpredictably between Tanzania and Kenya. Nile crocodiles, steep banks and waiting predators create the migration’s most intense scenes.

  • Prime Maasai Mara migration window
  • Stay in private conservancies for quieter viewing
  • Book 9–15 months ahead for top camps
Request This Window
November – December

The Short-Rains Retreat

Fresh grass draws the herds south from the Mara through eastern Serengeti. The pace quickens, skies become cinematic, and travelers enjoy wide landscapes with fewer vehicles.

  • Excellent photography conditions
  • Less crowded than crossing season
  • Ideal for Kenya–Tanzania combinations
