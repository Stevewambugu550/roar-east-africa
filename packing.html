Savanna-Ready Apparel

Breathable layers in practical, muted colors.

Field note: Neutral colors are practical. Avoid camouflage clothing where it is restricted, and check the rules for every country on your itinerary.

Optics & Camera Gear

Prepare for distance, dust, and limited charging time.

Field note: Keep one camera ready, but put equipment away between sightings to limit dust exposure.

Luggage & Bush Essentials

Pack soft, light, and easy to move.

Flight note: Bush-flight allowances vary by operator and route and are often around 15 kg in soft bags. Follow the confirmed allowance on your ticket.

Documents & Health

Keep essentials accessible and backed up.

Health note: Consult a qualified travel-health professional about vaccinations, malaria prevention, and personal medication well before departure.
Before You Zip the Bag

Check your final flight documents.

Your confirmed itinerary and airline ticket—not a general website—are the authority for baggage dimensions, weight limits, restricted items, and battery handling.
