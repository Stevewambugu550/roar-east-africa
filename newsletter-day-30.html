The Science of a Flying Safari

Kenya’s landscapes are extraordinary, but transfers between regions can be long. The right domestic flight can preserve energy and turn a travel day into more time in camp.

Our fly-in designs connect Nairobi’s Wilson Airport with bush airstrips near the Mara, Amboseli, Samburu, and the coast. Flight times, baggage limits, routings, and schedules vary, so we build every connection around confirmed operations.
