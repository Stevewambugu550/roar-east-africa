Booking Terms & Conditions

Clear expectations for planning and operating your private East African journey.

Important

These terms are a working launch draft. Roar East Africa should have them reviewed by qualified Kenyan legal counsel and aligned with each lodge, airline, payment provider, and insurer before accepting deposits.

Last updated: September 2026
01

Booking and Contract Formation

A safari inquiry, proposal, or planning estimate does not create a confirmed reservation. A booking becomes binding only after Roar East Africa issues written confirmation, receives the stated deposit in cleared funds, and confirms the availability of all material services. The person making the booking confirms that they have authority to accept these terms for every traveler named in the booking.

02

Pricing and Estimates

Website calculator figures and preliminary proposals are planning estimates in United States dollars unless stated otherwise. Final pricing may change based on travel dates, exchange rates, park and conservancy fees, taxes, room configuration, flight availability, and supplier rates. No price is guaranteed until a written quotation is accepted and the required deposit is received.

03

Deposits and Final Payment

The deposit percentage and payment schedule will be stated in the individual quotation. Unless that quotation says otherwise, a 30% deposit is required to request confirmation and the final balance is due 60 days before arrival. Some lodges, charter flights, gorilla permits, peak-season rooms, and special services may require earlier or fully non-refundable payment. Those conditions will be disclosed before payment.

04

Cancellation by the Traveler

Cancellations must be submitted in writing. Refund eligibility is determined by the cancellation schedule in the accepted quotation and the non-refundable charges already imposed by suppliers. Bank, card, currency-conversion, permit, and administrative costs may be deducted from any refundable amount. Travelers should obtain comprehensive cancellation insurance when booking.

05

Changes and Cancellation by Roar East Africa

Safari operations may require reasonable itinerary changes because of weather, road conditions, wildlife movements, government action, safety concerns, flight schedules, or supplier availability. Where a material service becomes unavailable, Roar East Africa will seek a comparable alternative where reasonably possible. Any applicable remedy will depend on the circumstances, supplier rules, and mandatory consumer law.

06

Wildlife and Migration Disclaimer

Wild animals move freely and sightings, river crossings, weather, and natural events cannot be guaranteed. Guides use current field knowledge to maximize viewing opportunities, but the absence of a particular species or event does not constitute non-performance of the booked safari.

07

Travel Documents and Health

Travelers are responsible for valid passports, visas, vaccination documentation, entry requirements, and professional medical advice. Guests must disclose relevant dietary, mobility, health, or accessibility needs before confirmation so suitability can be assessed. Roar East Africa cannot guarantee that every remote camp or activity can accommodate every requirement.

08

Travel and Medical Insurance

Every traveler must maintain comprehensive travel insurance covering trip cancellation, interruption, medical treatment, emergency evacuation, repatriation, personal belongings, and the planned activities for the full journey. Proof of suitable coverage may be requested before travel.

09

Traveler Conduct and Safety

Travelers must follow reasonable safety instructions from guides, pilots, camp personnel, park authorities, and Roar East Africa representatives. A traveler may be removed from an activity or itinerary if their conduct creates a material safety risk, disrupts other guests, damages property, or violates applicable law. Resulting costs remain the traveler’s responsibility.

10

Suppliers and Liability

Accommodation providers, airlines, charter operators, park authorities, and activity providers are independent suppliers. Roar East Africa will exercise reasonable care when selecting and coordinating services but is not responsible for events outside its reasonable control. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limits rights available under applicable consumer law.

11

Force Majeure

Neither party is responsible for failure caused by extraordinary circumstances beyond reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disaster, epidemic, conflict, civil unrest, border closure, government restriction, industrial action, or transport disruption. The parties will work in good faith to minimize impact, subject to safety, supplier terms, and recoverable funds.

12

Personal Information

Traveler information is used to prepare quotations, administer bookings, coordinate suppliers, meet legal obligations, and provide travel support. Only information reasonably necessary for those purposes should be shared. Payment-card information should be submitted only through an approved secure payment provider, never through ordinary email or messaging.

13

Complaints and Governing Terms

Concerns should be reported immediately during travel so the local team has an opportunity to resolve them. Any unresolved complaint should be submitted in writing promptly after the journey. The final version of these terms must identify Roar East Africa’s registered legal entity, physical address, governing law, dispute forum, and official customer-support contact before payments are accepted.
