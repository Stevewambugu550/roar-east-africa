Our Story

East African roots.
World-class standards.

Roar East Africa is a Kenya-founded luxury safari company that designs private, purpose-led journeys across East Africa's most remarkable landscapes.

Why we exist

We believe the best safaris are personal.

Every guest arrives with a different dream: a first glimpse of a black-maned lion, a quiet sunrise over the Mara, a multi-generational reunion beneath Kilimanjaro, or a honeymoon that feels like a private expedition.

Our role is to listen, design, and deliver with the precision of a luxury travel house and the warmth of a family-run African company. We do not sell pre-packaged departures. We build journeys around you, your pace, your people, and the season you are travelling in.

How we travel

The principles behind every itinerary

Transparent Planning

Clear inclusions, realistic expectations, and no payment request before services are confirmed. You know what you are booking before you commit.

Local Leadership

Founded and led by East Africans who know the terrain, the camps, and the communities. That local intelligence shapes every route.

Thoughtful Impact

Lower-impact journey design, community-minded partners, and conservation-aware choices that help protect the places we visit.

Personal Accountability

Direct access to your planning team from first inquiry to departure. One consistent point of contact, not an anonymous call centre.

From Kenya to the World

A company built on first-hand knowledge.

Roar East Africa was created to raise the standard of East African safari travel: more personal, more transparent, and more responsive than the big international operators.

We have walked the conservancies, tested the lodges, and built relationships with the guides, pilots, and camp managers who make a safari exceptional. That first-hand knowledge is what allows us to match the right guest to the right experience.

Whether you are planning a celebration, a family adventure, a photography expedition, or a once-in-a-lifetime migration trip, we treat your journey as if it were our own.
